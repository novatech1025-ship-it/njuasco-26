Our Journey

A Rich History of Achievement

Founded in 1953 in New Juaben, Koforidua, NJUASCO has grown from humble beginnings into one of Ghana's respected senior high schools. With the motto "HARDWORK," we consistently produce graduates who excel in academia, public service, and business — making lasting impact on Ghana and the world.
